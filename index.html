    8 July 2026
    Day 79

    Build Tape — A Vintage Cassette for 79 Days

    Day 79 ships the Build Tape: a fully-rendered cassette deck for the 79-day build streak. A side-A label reads AJH // 2026, the reels actually spin when you press play (left reel's spokes rotate clockwise, right reel counter-clockwise at a speed that matches the build tempo), and the magnetic ribbon between them slides left-to-right as each track plays. The deck has a window with the spinning reels visible, a chrome transport head, a 3-digit mechanical counter that ticks up with each track, and a Side A / Side B label that flips when you run out of room. Space toggles play, ←/→ jumps prev/next, Shift+←/→ jumps ±5 tracks, Flip side splits the tape into two halves (Days 1–40 / 41–79), and a real shuffle mode plays a randomized order. The Now Playing card shows the current build's day, name, archetype tag, date, and a progress bar; the tracklist below lists all 79 builds with a pulsing dot on the active track and impact dots tinted by archetype. Stat tiles: 79 Tracks, total impact, now-playing day, and the runtime clock. Auto-advance at end of side, localStorage remembers the last position and side, JSON export dumps the full tape. The build has a soundtrack now — and it's playing.

    7 July 2026
    Day 78

    Build Garden — 8 Plants Growing for 77 Days

    Day 78 ships the Build Garden: a living plot where every archetype is a plant and every shipped day is a watering. Eight species take root across two rows of four — Rose (Visual, hot pink petals), Sunflower (Audio, towering gold), Bonsai (Systems, sculpted trunk), Mushroom (Interactive, glowing cap), Lotus (Data, floating blossom), Cactus (Meta, spiny and stoic), Oak (Craft, broad canopy), and Vine (Social, sprawling tendrils). Each plant is SVG-drawn, scales with the most-recent build in that archetype, and is tinted by impact (drought → sprout → bud → bloom). Stat tiles: 8 species, in-bloom count, watered-today count, and a season indicator that rolls with the build week. Water All triggers a rain animation, New Day advances time and blooms the latest plant, Export dumps the full snapshot as ajh-garden-YYYY-MM-DD.json. A 14-day "Recent Plantings" feed lists the last builds with their species, date, and impact dots. Click any plant to open a detail panel with archetype stats, last-7-day growth log, and a "Read in build log" link. localStorage persistence, light theme, mobile responsive. Press G G to open — the build has soil now.

    6 July 2026
    Day 77

    Build Weather — Every Day Has a Forecast

    Day 77 ships the Build Weather: a forecast engine that turns the 77-day build history into a 14-day outlook. Each shipped day is now a weather report — peak Sunny for the highest-impact days, Partly Cloudy for the steady ones, and Stormy for the off days. The headline forecast is what today (Day 77, Build Weather) brings: a high-pressure system over the data archetype, a 94% chance of shipping, and a temp of 32.5°C. The 14-day outlook (Days 64–77) rolls up by day with hi/lo impact, conditions, wind, and a one-line reading. Filter chips switch between All / 6 weather types / 8 archetypes — chip pills with active state and a chip count. The Climate panel shows the season-by-season average: spring (cool & dry), summer (warm & stormy — the build tempo heats up). Stat tiles: 77 days observed, 7-day streak, 14-day forecast, current temp. The "Current Conditions" card shows a giant animated weather icon for today's condition with wind, humidity (impact%), pressure (build load), and a 5-day mini forecast. The day list shows last 7 days with condition pills, wind bar, and impact thermometer. JSON export, localStorage persistence, light theme, mobile responsive. The build has weather now — and the forecast looks Sunny.

    5 July 2026
    Day 76

    Build Trail — 76 Days Laid End to End

    Day 76 ships the Build Trail: a horizontal chronological ribbon of every shipped build day — the third lens on the same data after the Constellation Map (sky) and Build DNA (genome). 76 day cards laid out in time order, grouped by month with era labels (April, May, June, July), each card showing day number, short date, build name, archetype icon, and a 5-dot impact meter. Massive days get a glow ring, today's card pulses on the rail, and a top-builds leaderboard crowns the highest-impact days. Mouse wheel and arrow keys scroll the rail sideways, T flies to today, Start/End jump to the boundaries, filter chips narrow by archetype, and every card opens a full detail panel with the build's full name, date, archetype, impact, and a Read in build log link. Progress bar tracks horizontal scroll, JSON export dumps the full snapshot, and localStorage remembers the filter, visits, and view position. 76 days laid end to end — the shape of the build, in time.

    4 July 2026
    Day 75

    Constellation Map — 75 Days as a Starfield

    Day 75 ships the Constellation Map: every build day rendered as a star in an interactive sky. 75 stars spread across 8 archetype clusters (Systems, Visual, Audio, Interactive, Data, Meta, Craft, Social), each one sized by impact and tinted by archetype. Massive days glow brighter, recent days pulse on hover, and a constellation of faint lines traces the path of the build through time. Click any star to read that day's build in a side panel — date, archetype, impact, magnitude, share-this-star. Filter chips switch between All 75, Last 10, Big Days, Massive, and per-archetype views. A fireworks Burst mode spawns colored spark rays on top stars every 1.6s. Stats card shows Total Stars, Avg Impact, Massive Days, Big Days, and a persistent visits counter. JSON export, keyboard nav, light theme, mobile responsive. Press Esc to close the panel, or jump from the command palette (G S).

    3 July 2026
    Day 74

    Build DNA — Your Pattern Genome

    Day 74 ships Build DNA: a living fingerprint of the build. A two-strand SVG helix renders 8 builder archetypes (Systems, Visual, Audio, Craft, Meta, Data, Experiment, Speed) as colored nucleotides — each strand a profile of the build's actual character. The DNA auto-computes from your tag history, recent features, archetype dominance, and rhythm: a 4-card summary shows Strand, Personality, Cadence, and Diversity. The Personality card surfaces one of 6 archetypes (Polymath, Craftsperson, Audio Hacker, Meta Builder, Steady Hand, Wild Card) based on which DNA strand is loudest. An 8-bar Archetype Breakdown ranks each category by % of build time, with a 1-5 dominance score. A legend explains every color, a focus card highlights the most under-built or most-needed area, and a build log feed shows the last 12 builds with their DNA sparkline. Press N in the section to recompute, or open from the command palette.

    2 July 2026
    Day 73

    Lab Notebook — Hypothesis Log for Builders

    Day 73 ships the Lab Notebook: a build hypothesis log where every bet gets a write-up. Each experiment has a title, a one-line if I add X, then Y hypothesis, a result, a 1-5 confidence rating, a status (Draft → Running → Validated / Falsified / Parked), a date, and tag chips. The Library tab lists everything with status filters, a tag search, and a sort selector. The Board tab shows a 3-column Now / Validated / Parked kanban so you can see what's actually live vs. what's shipped. Four summary tiles track Total experiments, Validated, Running, and your Kill Rate. Every card has a one-click Share (tweet-sized text), Copy, and an inline Advance button that walks the experiment through Draft → Running → Validated / Falsified / Parked. JSON import/export, reset-to-seed, and full localStorage persistence. Press L in the section to jump in, or open from the command palette.

    1 July 2026
    Day 72

    The Forge — A Build Reflection Studio

    Day 72 ships The Forge: a Build Reflection Studio that turns each day's ship into a tiny ritual. Rate your build on five axes (Difficulty, Pride, Reach, Polish, Fun), write a 280-character note about what shipped / broke / surprised you, and stamp it with one of six tags (Ship, Fix, Design, Tool, Meta, Milestone) and a mood emoji (🔥 💡 🛠️ 🎯 🤯 😴). The Forge shows your running weekly average, a 7-day mini-heatmap of reflection energy, the day-of-year average across all 72 builds, and the longest reflection streak. Each entry is shareable as a tweet or downloadable as a JSON card. Reflections persist to localStorage and are private to you — no server, no tracking. Press F in the section to jump to the write-card.

    30 June 2026
    Day 71

    Step Sequencer — A Beat Machine in the Browser

    Day 71 ships the Step Sequencer: an 8-track × 16-step drum machine that drives the Web Audio API. Each row is a soundboard-style synth (Kick, Snare, Hat, Clap, Tom, Cowbell, Crash, Tom-Hi), and each column is a sixteenth note. Toggle steps by clicking, drag to paint multiple cells, and press Space to play / stop. BPM runs 60–200 with a swing knob, master volume and per-track mute/solo/volume, four pattern banks, save/load named patterns, share-as-URL, JSON export/import, and a playhead that scrolls across the grid. Patterns persist to localStorage; the URL hash lets you share a pattern with a friend.

    29 June 2026
    Day 70

    Soundboard — Pocket Studio in the Browser

    Day 70 ships the Soundboard: a 24-pad soundboard built on the Web Audio API, with no audio files — everything is synthesized in real time. Six categories (UI, Arcade, Synth, Nature, Retro, Voice) plus filters and favorites. Each pad shows a live waveform and a keyboard shortcut. Master controls for volume, reverb, playback speed, and oscillator wave. Press 1–9/0 to fire pads, Space to stop all, F to favorite. Plays, favorites, and preferences persist to localStorage.

Search across 68 days by title / tag / day number, filter by Featured / Ship / Design / Tools / Meta / Milestone, and step through days with ← / →. Stats (printed, shared, copied) persist to localStorage. Hit R in the section for a random receipt.

Hit Reroll if today's prompt isn't speaking to you, then Start Drawing to jump straight into the Pixel Art Studio with the challenge name pre-filled. Press D in the section to reroll, or open from the command palette.
