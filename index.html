    Daily Build Log

    New features, improvements, and experiments

    28 June 2026
    Day 69

    Build Receipts — Print Your Streak

    Day 69 ships Build Receipts: a printable, shareable receipt for any day of the build. Pick today, dig up day 1, or hit Random for a surprise. Each receipt is styled like classic thermal paper — perforated edges, monospace font, barcode, dotted dividers, dotted impact meter. Print, Copy as Text, Share via Web Share, or Download a .txt file. Search across 68 days by title / tag / day number, filter by Featured / Ship / Design / Tools / Meta / Milestone, and step through days with ← / →. Stats (printed, shared, copied) persist to localStorage. Hit R in the section for a random receipt.

    27 June 2026
    Day 68

    Daily Pixel Challenge — 250 Prompts, One Per Day

    Day 68 ships the Daily Pixel Challenge: a rotating daily prompt that pairs with the Pixel Art Studio. Today's challenge auto-loads based on day-of-year and stays the same all day, sourced from a 250-prompt bank across 8 categories (Object, Creature, Food, Scene, Icon, Character, Nature, Tech). Hit Reroll if today's prompt isn't speaking to you, then Start Drawing to jump straight into the Pixel Art Studio with the challenge name pre-filled. Streak counter, completion tracking, and prompt history persist to localStorage; share today's challenge to social or export the whole log as JSON. Press D in the section to reroll, or open from the command palette.

    26 June 2026
    Day 67

    Pixel Art Studio — Paint 16×16 Masterpieces

    Day 67 ships the Pixel Art Studio: a full 16×16 pixel editor with paint / erase / fill / eyedropper tools, a 16-color palette, undo/redo stack, and one-click export to PNG. Save your pieces to a local gallery, share them as a base64 URL hash, or remix one of five hand-curated starter pieces (heart, star, lightning bolt, smiley, checker). Pixels painted, saved pieces, exports, and stroke count all persist to localStorage. Click X in the command palette (or the palette icon in the hero meta) to jump straight in.

    25 June 2026
    Day 66

    On This Day — 365 Days of Builder Wisdom

    Day 66 ships On This Day: a daily-rotating wisdom card plus a swipeable 365-card deck of builder principles. Today's card auto-loads based on day-of-year (so it stays the same all day), with the option to swipe through every principle ever shipped, bookmark favorites, copy to clipboard, or share via Web Share API. Filter by category (Engineering / Craft / Discipline / Mindset / Shipping), keyboard arrows to navigate, and a counter shows progress through the year. Cards are seeded from a 365-entry bank covering everything from "delete is the cheapest feature" to "ship the smallest useful thing today". All bookmarks persist to localStorage. Click O in the command palette to jump straight in.

    24 June 2026
    Day 64

    Build Journal — Shipped / Learned / Broke

    Day 64 ships the Build Journal: a structured daily log with three columns — Shipped, Learned, Broke — plus a one-line headline and a mood picker. A weekly ring shows the last 7 days at a glance (logged = filled, empty = outline), and a side panel lists recent entries with quick jump-back. One-click Save persists everything to localStorage with a date-keyed JSON store; a copy-to-clipboard share button turns today's entry into a tweet-sized summary. Days Building jumps to 64, the journal summary tracks streak and total ships. The streak counter is now fed by the journal, so a missed day would visibly interrupt the ring — designed to make breaking the streak painful.

    23 June 2026
    Day 63

    Reading Mode & Reading List — Distraction-Free Read

    Day 63 shipped a Reading Mode and a personal Reading List. Press R (or click the book icon in the hero meta) to enter distraction-free reading: chrome fades out, type scales up, line-height opens, and a per-section progress bar tracks how far through the section you are. Add any section to your Reading List to save it for later — word count and estimated read time are calculated for every section automatically. Print any section with Ctrl/⌘ + P using the new print stylesheet. Stats (sections read, words consumed, list size) persist to localStorage.

    22 June 2026
    Day 62

    Theme Studio — Reskin the Site Live

    Day 62 shipped a Theme Studio: live CSS-variable customizer with color pickers for background, surface, primary / secondary / tertiary accents, font family, border radius, density, animation speed, and a motion toggle. Six named presets ship with the studio (Default Midnight, Aurora, Sunset, Mono, Synthwave, Forest), plus save-your-own themes that persist to localStorage, share-as-URL hash, and a randomize button. Press T to jump to the studio.

    21 June 2026
    Day 61

    Time Capsule Vault — Letters to Future-Me

    Day 61 added a Time Capsule Vault. Compose a note to future-you, pick the unlock date, and seal it. The vault shows live countdowns for every sealed capsule, mood tags for the journey, and a "Next Unlock" tile that ticks down in real time. Open an unlocked capsule to read what past-you wrote. Press T to jump straight to the vault.

    20 June 2026
    Day 60

    Site Constellation — 25 Sections, One Graph

    Sixty days straight. To mark it I added a constellation view: every section on this site laid out as a node in an interactive 2D graph, with edges drawn between related sections. Drag nodes around, zoom and pan, search by name, filter by category, and click any node to jump. The graph auto-arranges on load, the connected node of whatever you're hovering pulses, and you can reset the layout any time. A new way to see the whole site at once.

    19 June 2026
    Day 59

    Bookmark Cards — Deep-Link Any Section

    Every section on this site now has a generated share card — title, icon, description, and a one-click copy link. Browse in grid or list, search across all 25 cards, pin the ones you return to, and pop open the detail modal to share via the native share sheet (or clipboard fallback). Click any card to jump straight to the section. Press B to jump to Bookmarks.

    18 June 2026
    Day 58

    Build Assistant — Chat With the Streak

    A chatbot that lives inside the site and knows about every one of the 58 build days. Press A anywhere to open it, or hit the robot button in the bottom-right. Ask by day number, by date, by tag, or just say "recent" or "biggest". The assistant is pure JavaScript, pattern-matches against the same build data the calendar uses, and saves the conversation locally. It's Day 58 shipping Day 58, which feels right.

    17 June 2026
    Day 57

    Build Calendar Heatmap — 57 Days Visible

    A GitHub-style contribution graph for the build streak. 57 cells, color-coded by impact, with click-to-read modals for every single day. Includes summary stats (days built, current/longest streak, biggest drop), filter views (all / last 30 / featured), month labels, weekday axis, and a 5-step legend. Scroll up to see 57 squares of evidence that the streak is real.

    A personal snippet library right inside the site. Filter by language, search by title or content, copy with one click, and add your own. Comes seeded with ten hand-picked snippets I keep reaching for, and lives in localStorage so your library survives every refresh. Open with the command palette or click the Snippets nav link.

The hero meta now has a checklist button that scrolls straight to the plan.
